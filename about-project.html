About Eco Watt Monitor

Smart Energy Monitoring for Sustainable Homes

Project Overview

Eco Watt Monitor is a household energy monitoring project designed to help users understand their electricity consumption and reduce unnecessary usage.

Project Objectives

  • Track daily, weekly and monthly electricity usage.
  • Understand energy consumption by appliances.
  • Calculate estimated electricity costs.
  • Encourage energy-saving habits.
  • Support sustainable living.

Key Features

Energy tracking, appliance monitoring, energy charts, electricity cost calculation, saving targets and consumption warnings.

Project Benefits

The project helps households make informed decisions about electricity use, potentially reduce energy bills and support environmental sustainability.
