Smart Sustainability Tips

Swipe through personalised ideas to lower your energy use.

Tip 01 • Peak hours

Move heavy appliance use away from peak time.

Running washing machines, dryers, or dishwashers earlier or later can reduce strain during high-demand periods and may lower costs on time-of-use plans.

🕒
Tip 02 • Standby power

Switch off devices that quietly use power all day.

TVs, gaming consoles, chargers, and desktop equipment can still draw electricity while idle. Use a switched power board to disconnect several devices at once.

🔌
Tip 03 • Cooling

Adjust the thermostat before increasing fan speed.

A small temperature adjustment can noticeably reduce heating or cooling demand. Track the impact in the live chart after changing your settings.
