Understanding Household Energy

What Is Energy Consumption?

Energy consumption is the amount of electricity used by household appliances and devices.

Why Monitor Electricity?

Monitoring electricity helps households understand their usage, reduce waste and lower electricity bills.

Ways to Save Energy

  • Switch off unused lights.
  • Choose energy-efficient appliances.
  • Reduce unnecessary air conditioning.
  • Track electricity consumption regularly.
